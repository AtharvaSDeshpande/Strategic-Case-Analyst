# PESTEL

Accumulated guidance on applying the macro-environment scan well. Definitions belong in the
per-case concept note; this page holds method.

## How to apply this well

- Treat each category as a question about direction and pace, not as a slot to fill with a noun. A
  factor earns its place when you can say which way it is moving and on what timetable.
- Connect every factor you keep to a specific competitive force or a specific firm decision. A
  macro observation with no downstream link is background, not analysis.
- Macro forces rarely land evenly across an industry. Segment before generalising: the same shift
  can reshape one segment and barely touch another.
- Leave a category visibly empty when the evidence does not reach it. An empty cell is information;
  a filled cell with nothing behind it is noise.
- Watch for the category that is also an actor. Where the state sets input prices, supply and entry
  conditions, the political and legal categories are not context, they are the main event.

## Signals that the application is weak

- Six categories, each with a generic sentence that would fit any industry.
- Factors listed with no direction, no timing and no magnitude.
- No link between any factor and any force or decision.
