# Preparatory Notes

## 1. Company Selection
- **Retail: Costco.** Picked over Walmart or Target because of its highly specific, non-traditional business model (membership fees rather than retail margins) which makes for starker capability contrasts.
- **IT: Netflix.** Picked over Apple or Microsoft due to its singular, rapid pivot from logistics (DVDs) to digital infrastructure (streaming) to content production, making it ideal for environmental adaptation models.
- **FMCG: Nestle.** Picked over Procter & Gamble because of its aggressive recent portfolio restructuring (divesting U.S. candy, acquiring health science firms), providing clearer concrete actions for growth and allocation models.

## 2. Core Capabilities
- **Costco:** Supply Chain & Logistics (specifically bulk procurement and membership retention).
- **Netflix:** Content Delivery & Data Analytics (predictive streaming and original content targeting).
- **Nestle:** Global Brand Portfolio Management (allocating capital across disparate food/beverage/health categories).

## 3. Selected Frameworks (6 Unique)
- **Costco:** Porter's 5 Forces, Corporate Scope
- **Netflix:** S.E. Model (Strategy & Environment), Porter's Value Chain
- **Nestle:** BCG Matrix, Ansoff Matrix
