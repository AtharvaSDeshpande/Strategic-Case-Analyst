# Strategic Case Analyst - Final Report

## 1. Companies Chosen & Rationale
- **Retail: Costco.** Picked over Walmart or Target due to its specific membership-driven model and massive scale leverage.
- **IT: Netflix.** Picked over Apple or Microsoft because of its profound pivot from physical to digital logistics, perfect for structural and environmental analysis.
- **FMCG: Nestle.** Picked over Procter & Gamble due to its aggressive portfolio pruning and strategic divestments (like US Candy in 2018).

## 2. Core Capabilities Identified
- **Costco:** Supply Chain & Logistics (Volume/Membership retention)
- **Netflix:** Content Delivery & Data Analytics
- **Nestle:** Global Brand Portfolio Management (Capital Allocation)

## 3. Frameworks Selected
1. **Costco - Porter's 5 Forces:** Fits smoothly because Costco's restricted SKU strategy fundamentally reverses supplier power.
2. **Costco - Corporate Scope:** Kirkland Signature is the perfect lens for horizontal/vertical integration acting as a ceiling on external pricing.
3. **Netflix - S.E. Model:** The 2007 shift from DVD to streaming is a textbook response to an evolving broadband environment.
4. **Netflix - Value Chain:** The $17B original content spend is a direct move upstream to prevent legacy studios from capturing the value.
5. **Nestle - BCG Matrix:** Divesting the US Candy business ($2.8B) perfectly illustrates managing a 'Dog' to free capital for 'Stars'.
6. **Nestle - Ansoff Matrix:** The Aimmune Therapeutics acquisition ($2.6B) is a clear example of moving into high-margin adjacencies (Product/Market penetration).
