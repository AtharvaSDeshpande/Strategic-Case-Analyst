# Panel Notes (Isolated Lenses)

## Costco - Porter's 5 Forces
- **Fit:** Perfectly explains how Costco survives on razor-thin margins. By stripping away choices and buying in extreme bulk, they flip the script on supplier power. 
- **Anecdote:** Costco's buyers dictate package sizes and pricing to major brands because of their volume. 
- **Ledger ID:** C1 (Scale of revenue enables this).

## Costco - Corporate Scope
- **Fit:** Costco integrates vertically into product creation when brands won't play ball or when margins on staples are too high.
- **Anecdote:** Kirkland Signature generated $58B in 2023, effectively replacing countless external suppliers with an internal brand that ensures quality while capping prices.
- **Ledger ID:** C1.

## Netflix - S.E. Model (Strategy & Environment)
- **Fit:** Shows a company whose environment shifted radically (from postal delivery to broadband, then from licensed content to original studios).
- **Anecdote:** Introduction of streaming in 2007 cannibalized their own profitable DVD business before rivals could do it, aligning their strategy with the new broadband environment.
- **Ledger ID:** N1.

## Netflix - Porter's Value Chain
- **Fit:** Netflix realized that owning the delivery platform wasn't enough if studios pulled their content. They expanded upstream into the value chain (content production).
- **Anecdote:** Committing $17 billion to original content in 2024 to ensure they aren't held hostage by legacy studios like Disney or Warner Bros.
- **Ledger ID:** N2.

## Nestle - BCG Matrix
- **Fit:** Nestle manages hundreds of brands and actively prunes them based on cash flow and market growth.
- **Anecdote:** They divested their U.S. candy business (including Butterfinger) to Ferrero for $2.8B in 2018 because it was an underperforming "dog" trailing behind Hershey and Mars.
- **Ledger ID:** NES1.

## Nestle - Ansoff Matrix
- **Fit:** Nestle uses aggressive acquisition to penetrate new, higher-margin adjacent markets when legacy categories stagnate.
- **Anecdote:** Acquiring Aimmune Therapeutics for $2.6B to push into the medical nutrition and food allergy space, shifting away from standard grocery aisles.
- **Ledger ID:** NES2.
