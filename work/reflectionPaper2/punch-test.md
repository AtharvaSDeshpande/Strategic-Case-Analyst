# Punch Test Scores

## IKEA
**Anecdote 1 (Value Chain):** Target pricing and flat-packing.
- **Non-obvious:** Yes, shows that price isn't an afterthought but the starting constraint of design.
- **Concrete:** Yes, specifically designing to eliminate dead air in packaging.
- **Legible without label:** Yes, the logic of offloading labor and transport costs is universally understood.
- **Decision:** KEEP.

**Anecdote 2 (Corporate Scope):** Vertical integration vs horizontal expansion.
- **Non-obvious:** Most see IKEA as a retailer, but owning forests and factories defines their scope.
- **Concrete:** Buying forests in Eastern Europe to secure wood supply.
- **Legible without label:** Yes, securing raw materials to maintain the low-price guarantee.
- **Decision:** KEEP.

## NVIDIA
**Anecdote 3 (5 Forces):** The lock-in of CUDA.
- **Non-obvious:** People think NVIDIA wins on hardware speed, but the real moat is software muscle memory.
- **Concrete:** Researchers unable to migrate because rewriting kernels takes too many engineering hours.
- **Legible without label:** Yes, switching costs and ecosystem gravity are clear.
- **Decision:** KEEP.

**Anecdote 4 (Aghion-Howitt):** Planned obsolescence via rapid innovation.
- **Non-obvious:** Usually companies protect their cash cows, but NVIDIA actively destroys theirs with the next generation.
- **Concrete:** Releasing new architectures every two years.
- **Legible without label:** Yes, the necessity of cannibalizing your own sales to stay ahead.
- **Decision:** KEEP.

## Unilever
**Anecdote 5 (BCG Matrix):** Pruning the portfolio.
- **Non-obvious:** Scale is usually seen as universally good, but unmanaged scale breeds complexity and drags down margins.
- **Concrete:** Cutting 1,200 brands to focus on 400 core ones.
- **Legible without label:** Yes, feeding the winners and starving the losers.
- **Decision:** KEEP.

**Anecdote 6 (Ansoff Matrix):** Pushing into Prestige Beauty.
- **Non-obvious:** Shows growth isn't just about selling more soap, but entering entirely new, higher-margin categories.
- **Concrete:** Reorganizing into 5 business groups and acquiring premium wellness brands.
- **Legible without label:** Yes, seeking new pools of profit outside legacy categories.
- **Decision:** KEEP.
