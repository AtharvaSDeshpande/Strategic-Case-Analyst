# Structure-Conduct-Performance

Accumulated guidance on tracing industry structure through firm behaviour into results.

## How to apply this well

- Walk the chain explicitly. The value of the framework is that it forces you to say *how* a
  structural feature reaches the income statement, rather than asserting that it does.
- Treat the flow as two-way. Strong performance funds conduct, such as sustained heavy investment,
  which can reshape structure in the incumbent's favour. A strictly one-directional reading misses
  the most interesting cases.
- It is particularly useful where a fragmented or informal market is being formalised, because the
  conduct of the largest players is actively creating the structure the framework normally treats as
  given.
- Keep conduct distinct from performance. Pricing behaviour is conduct; the margin it produces is
  performance. Collapsing them makes the chain unfalsifiable.

## Signals that the application is weak

- Structure and performance asserted with no conduct in between.
- Conduct described in terms of intentions rather than observable behaviour.
