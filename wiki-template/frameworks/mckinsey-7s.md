# McKinsey 7S

Accumulated guidance on diagnosing execution friction.

## How to apply this well

- Look for the **tension, not the inventory**. Seven paragraphs describing seven elements is not a
  diagnosis. Name at least one pair that conflicts and explain how the conflict blocks execution.
- A **hard-element change without soft-element support stalls**. Mandating a new structure or
  standard while people, incentives and shared assumptions still reward the old way produces passive
  non-compliance, which is harder to detect and slower to surface than open refusal.
- Expect **disclosure-based evidence to reach only the hard elements**. Financial and regulatory
  filings contain no headcount, attrition, decision rights or stated values, so three of the seven
  are typically invisible. Report them as not evidenced and name the source type that would show
  them. Filling them in from results is the exact error the model exists to prevent.
- Look for **misalignment inside the reporting system itself**: a new strategy whose performance
  cannot be seen because the new activity is reported inside an aggregate. That is a systems versus
  strategy conflict that only this framework tends to surface.
- Strong results establish capability; they do not establish its source. They cannot distinguish
  organisational excellence from a structural advantage that would have produced the same numbers.

## Signals that the application is weak

- All seven elements described regardless of evidence.
- Culture inferred from financial performance.
