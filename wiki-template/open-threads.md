# Open threads

Tensions, contradictions and unresolved questions surfaced by a run's cross-run comparison, or by a
disagreement between two sources that was not settled. Appended during Ingest. Marked resolved in
place when later work genuinely resolves them. Never deleted.

Suggested entry shape:

```
## <the tension, in one sentence>
**Raised by:** <run id>
**Status:** open | resolved (<run id>)
**Detail:** <what conflicts with what, and why it matters>
```
